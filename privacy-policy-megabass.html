Mega Bass Privacy Policy

Effective September 18, 2026. This policy explains how Mega Bass handles information when you use the app.

Information the app may process

Mega Bass may process device identifiers, product interactions, advertising data, purchase history, crash data, performance data, and other diagnostics. Audio recordings and imported files remain on your device unless you choose to share them using system features.

How information is used

Information is used to operate app features, restore purchases and premium access, measure performance, diagnose crashes, understand product usage, deliver advertising, prevent abuse, and improve Mega Bass.

Advertising and tracking

Mega Bass uses Google Mobile Ads and may request App Tracking Transparency permission. If you do not allow tracking, the app can continue to show ads with reduced personalization. Consent choices may also be collected through Google User Messaging Platform where required.

Analytics and diagnostics

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics may collect app interactions, device information, performance information, and crash diagnostics to help us improve reliability and the product experience.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging process device tokens needed to deliver messages to your device. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and auto-renewable subscriptions are processed by Apple. Mega Bass receives transaction status and product information needed to unlock features and restore purchases, but does not receive your full payment-card details.

Third-party services

The app uses services provided by Apple, Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Firebase Cloud Messaging. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and applicable law.

Children's privacy

Mega Bass is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly request personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us so we can review the situation.

Security and retention

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure. Third-party providers retain information according to their policies and legal obligations.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. Questions can be sent to antonio.filho128@gmail.com.
